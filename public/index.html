DERAIL

one story. every player has a different ending in mind.

or

how this works
  1. The engine drops a boring opening scene. Everyone sees it.
  2. You're secretly assigned a ridiculous goal to steer the story toward.
  3. Take turns adding one sentence. 15 seconds each. Be subtle.
  4. Think you've spotted someone's agenda? Hit DERAIL and accuse them.
  5. Guess right → they're busted. Guess wrong → you lose your next turn.
  6. After 10 rounds, everyone's goals are revealed and the table votes on who pulled it off.
