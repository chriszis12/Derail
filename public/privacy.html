Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information Derail ("the game", "we") collects when you play, and why.

Account information

If you sign in with Google, Discord, or GitHub, we receive your name, avatar/profile photo, and a unique account ID from that provider. We never see or store your password — sign-in is handled entirely by the provider. If you play as a guest instead, no account information is collected.

Gameplay data

While you play: your chosen display name, avatar color, the sentences you write into the story, your score, and your room's chat-adjacent game state are visible to other players in your room and held in server memory for the duration of that game. This data is not permanently stored and does not survive a server restart.

Local storage / cookies

Your browser stores a few preferences locally (display name, language, sound settings) using localStorage, and a session cookie that identifies your browser to the server so you can rejoin a game after a refresh. These never leave your device except the session ID itself, which the server uses only to match you to your seat in a room.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads and read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

We use [Google AI/Gemini] to help judge whether a player achieved their goal at the end of a match, when that feature is enabled. The finished story text and each player's goal are sent to that service to generate a verdict; no account information is included in that request.
